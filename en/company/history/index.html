The milestones

Ten events, as KAO MING states them

KAO MING publishes ten years and ten milestones, but the carousel on their current site scrambled which event belongs to which year. Ten of each in the same order look exactly like ten pairs — which is why they are not printed as pairs here. The dates are being confirmed with KAO MING; the timeline pairs them the day they are.

  1. 01KAO MING Machinery Company set up by founder Mr. Chang, Gao-Ming and his wife Ms. Chang, Tsai Mei-Hsia, located on Chung Cheng Road, Feng Yuan City, Taichung County
  2. 02Change the company name to Kao Ming Industry Co.
  3. 03Reorganized as Kao Ming Machinery Industrial Co., Ltd.
  4. 04Relocated to the new headquarters
  5. 05Kao Ming is the first company to invest in a production line for assembling double-column machining centers
  6. 06First machine tool manufacturer in Taiwan to obtain ISO 9001; KMC-SD series obtained a Good Design award
  7. 07Announced gantry type machining center KMC-G series; two-step spindle in-line integrated design obtained Utility Model patent #M316769; Y-axis extension bellow obtained Utility Model patent #M322297
  8. 08Application for the plant in the Central Taiwan Science Park approved, area 25,000 square meters; built the constant-temperature plant
  9. 09Importing-in Toyota Production System
  10. 10Announced five-axis moving crossbeam & moving column KMC-EPG series

2023 = 55th anniversary (consistent with 1968 founding). 2023 EMO Hannover, Sept 18–23, was the last exhibition posted.

Years and events transcribed verbatim from kaoming.com, August 2026.
