Engineering

How it is built

  • 07

    Stable Column Structure

    Specially designed separated column structure combats structural deformation; columns reinforced with stiff interior ribs resist bending and torsional stress. Validated with Finite Element Analysis.

  • 08

    Dual-Speed Gear-Drive Spindle Head

    Thermally symmetrical in-line design with spindle and motor on the head centerline; coolant flows straight through motor, reducer and spindle. 26 kW (35HP) motor, 6000 rpm, max torque 670 Nm — heavy cutting down to 375 rpm. Vertical and horizontal machining via automatic indexing head.

  • 09

    Automatic Attachment Changer

    Standard 2-position AAC compartment; optional 3-to-4-position AAC. Head attachments exchange automatically for multi-face machining.

Gantry construction with FIXED table and TRAVELING crossbeam — the portal moves over the workpiece along X. Note for 3D: exploded-view and scroll choreography differ from table-moving machines.
